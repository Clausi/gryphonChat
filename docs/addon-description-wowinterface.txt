[size="5"][b]GryphonChat[/b][/size]

[b]A better chat for World of Warcraft: Retail, Classic Era, Anniversary and MoP Classic.[/b]

GryphonChat extends the default chat instead of replacing it. Everything stays where you know it, with a few tools that make chatting easier.

[size="4"][b]Features[/b][/size]

[size="3"][b]Clickable web links[/b][/size]
Web addresses in chat ([i]https://...[/i], [i]www....[/i]) become clickable links. One click opens a small window with the address selected: press [b]Ctrl+C[/b] and paste it into your browser.

[size="3"][b]Copy the chat[/b][/size]
A new button next to every chat window, including whisper windows, shows the whole chat of that window as plain text. Everything is selected right away, so [b]Ctrl+C[/b] copies it.

[size="3"][b]Longer chat history[/b][/size]
Chat windows normally keep only the last 128 lines. With [b]/gryphonchat lines <number>[/b] you choose how many lines they keep for the current character, up to 5000.

[size="3"][b]Short channel names[/b][/size]
Turn long channel names into short ones: [i][1. General - Elwynn Forest][/i] becomes [i][1. G][/i], [i][4. LookingForGroup][/i] becomes [i][4. LfG][/i]. Blizzard's channels come with short names, and you can give every channel your own, e.g. [b]/gryphonchat channels set Global G[/b]. Off by default, turn it on in the options or with [b]/gryphonchat channels on[/b].

[size="3"][b]Options[/b][/size]
All settings are on their own page in the game options ([b]Esc > Options > AddOns > GryphonChat[/b]). The short channel names can also be set right in the chat settings: right click on a chat tab, [b]Settings[/b], then [b]GryphonChat[/b].

[size="4"][b]Slash commands[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]/gryphonchat[/b] - Show all commands
[*][b]/gryphonchat options[/b] - Open the options
[*][b]/gryphonchat lines <50-5000>[/b] - How many lines every chat window of this character keeps (default 128)
[*][b]/gryphonchat channels[/b] - Show your channels and their short names
[*][b]/gryphonchat channels on|off[/b] - Short channel names for this character
[*][b]/gryphonchat channels set <channel> <short name>[/b] - Your own short name for a channel
[*][b]/gryphonchat channels reset <channel>[/b] - Remove your own short name
[*][b]/gryphonchat notify on|off[/b] - Turn the automatic messages of this character on or off
[/list]

[size="4"][b]Good to know[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Made for [b]Retail[/b], [b]Classic Era[/b], [b]Classic Anniversary[/b] and [b]MoP Classic[/b]. Support for [b]WoW Forever[/b] follows later.

[/list]
